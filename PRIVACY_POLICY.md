# Privacy Policy for Martial Arts Practice

**Last Updated: January 22, 2026**

## Introduction

This privacy policy explains how the Martial Arts Practice app ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

## Information We Collect

### Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The app stores the following information locally on your device:

- **Flashcard Content**: Questions, answers, and images you create or import
- **Study Progress**: Review history, scheduling data, and performance metrics
- **Settings**: Your preferences for theme, practice settings, and algorithm parameters
- **Practice Sessions**: Records of your practice sessions and grading history

**Important**: All of this data is stored exclusively on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or have access to any of this information.

### Information We Do NOT Collect

We do NOT collect, store, or transmit:
- Personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
- Device identifiers
- Location data
- Usage analytics or statistics
- Any data about your flashcards or study habits
- Cookies or tracking data

## Third-Party Services

### Google Play Store

The app is distributed through the Google Play Store, which is operated by Google LLC. Google may collect certain information as described in their privacy policy:
- https://policies.google.com/privacy

### Google Play Billing

If you choose to make a donation through the app, the transaction is processed by Google Play's in-app billing system. We do NOT:
- Store your payment information
- Have access to your credit card or payment details
- Collect any information about your donation

Google Play handles all payment processing securely. Please refer to Google's privacy policy for information about how they handle payment data:
- https://payments.google.com/payments/apis-secure/u/0/get_legal_document?ldo=0&ldt=privacynotice

### Data Shared with Third Parties

We do NOT share any data with third parties because we do not collect any data in the first place.

## Data Backup and Cloud Storage

The app uses Android's built-in backup features, which may back up your data to your Google account if you have enabled Android Auto Backup. This backup:
- Is encrypted
- Is controlled by Android system settings
- Can be disabled in your device settings (Settings → System → Backup)
- Is subject to Google's privacy policy

We do not have access to or control over this backup data.

## Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from children or anyone else. All data is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to us or any third party.

The app is appropriate for users of all ages, including children, as it contains no inappropriate content and collects no personal information.

## Data Security

Your data security is important to us:
- All flashcard and study data is stored locally on your device
- No data is transmitted over the internet (except for donation processing by Google)
- You have full control over your data
- You can export your data at any time through the app's export feature

## Your Data Rights

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control:

- **Access**: You can access all your data through the app at any time
- **Modification**: You can edit or update any flashcards or settings
- **Deletion**: You can delete individual cards, groups, or all data through the app
- **Export**: You can export your flashcards to CSV files
- **Complete Removal**: Uninstalling the app will remove all local data

## Data Retention

Data is retained on your device until you choose to delete it or uninstall the app. We do not retain any data on our servers because we do not collect any data.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be:
- Posted in this document
- Dated with the "Last Updated" date at the top
- Included in app updates

Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
